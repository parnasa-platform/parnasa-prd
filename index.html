01Purpose

Parnasa is an OTC platform in the UAE, regulated by VARA. Verified individuals and companies buy and sell BTC, ETH, USDT and USDC against AED and USD at a firm price for the full amount. Their funds are held with regulated partners, and Parnasa staff can show VARA that every customer and every movement was checked.

02Scope

CustomersIndividuals, and companies acting through a representative. Both use the same apps and have the same features unless a requirement names one of them.
AssetsBTC, ETH, USDT and USDC, traded against AED and USD.
TradingFirm quotes for the full amount, priced automatically from the liquidity providers with Parnasa's spread added, up to the maximum trade size. Above it, Parnasa's trading desk quotes by hand. There is no order book, and customers never trade with each other.
ApplicationsThe web app and the mobile app (iOS and Android) for OTC customers, the Back Office for Parnasa staff, and the backend that serves all three.
VendorsSumsub for KYC, KYB, AML screening and the Travel Rule. Ripple for custody and as liquidity provider 1. Zodia as liquidity provider 2. Elliptic for transaction monitoring and wallet screening. FusionX for banking.

Account statuses

OnboardingVerification not yet approved. The customer can sign in and complete onboarding.
ActiveVerification approved. The customer can use every feature.
FrozenRestricted by Parnasa. The customer can sign in, view balances and history, and receive deposits, but cannot trade or withdraw.
SuspendedAccess withdrawn by Parnasa. The customer cannot sign in, but deposits are still credited.
ClosedRelationship ended. The customer cannot sign in.

07Assumptions and dependencies

  1. Sumsub provides identity and company verification, sanctions, PEP and adverse media screening, and the exchange of Travel Rule information. Its verification flow is embedded in the web and mobile apps.
  2. Elliptic screens blockchain addresses and transactions.
  3. Ripple holds digital assets in custody. It issues a deposit address for each customer on each network, reports incoming deposits and their confirmations, and sends withdrawals.
  4. FusionX holds fiat. It assigns a virtual account number to each customer, reports incoming payments with the sender's name, sends outgoing payments and provides statements.
  5. Prices and trade execution come from Ripple as the first liquidity provider and Zodia as the second. When neither can price a pair, that pair cannot be traded.
  6. One-time codes for phone verification are sent by SMS through a messaging provider.
  7. Emails are sent through an outbound email service. Push notifications are delivered through the Apple and Google notification services.
  8. If a provider's test environment is not ready when the work that needs it starts, that part is built and shown against a simulated provider that behaves the same way, and switched over when access arrives. Bank transfers are expected to start this way.
  9. Parnasa's compliance and legal functions provide the list of restricted countries, the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy, the risk disclosure statement, the fee schedule, the complaints procedure, the published information and the onboarding questions.
  10. The Back Office is hosted on the same parent domain as the Parnasa API.
